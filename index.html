02. The Problem

Noise vs. Rhythm.

I discovered that the current interface broke the user's flow. I identified two extremes that guided my solution:

Elena, 22

Urban DJ

Buried in too many steps. I streamlined the 'Save' flow.
Result: Playlists curated in seconds.

Carlos, 70

Music Lover

Frustrated by visual noise. I isolated his personal library.
Result: Finds classics with zero errors.

[IMAGE: MODULAR INTERFACE / EDIT SPACE]

03. The Solution

Architectural Control.

To combat Decision Paralysis, I focused on architectural control rather than adding new features. By introducing a modular and editable card system, users can now customize their home screen.

The 14 original sections are now reduced and reorganized into a clearer, more hierarchical structure.

'Edit Your Space' feature: Users can now remove, reorder, or rename sections directly from the Home Screen.

Result:

Reduced cognitive load and faster access to desired content.
